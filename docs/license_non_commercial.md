# Academic Non-commercial Use

LSDetector is provided for academic research, education, peer review, and non-commercial disaster-science applications.

## Permitted Uses

You may use LSDetector to:

- reproduce academic experiments;
- evaluate LSDFormer and LSDSAM for landslide detection research;
- generate candidate landslide maps for non-commercial scientific studies;
- prepare theses, conference papers, journal papers, and research reports;
- teach remote-sensing, landslide detection, transfer learning, or geospatial AI workflows.

## Prohibited Uses

Without written permission from the authors, you may not:

- sell LSDetector or derivative software;
- provide paid commercial landslide detection services using LSDetector;
- integrate LSDetector into closed-source commercial products;
- redistribute model weights, datasets, or trained adapters under incompatible terms;
- remove citation, authorship, license, or non-commercial-use notices;
- use LSDetector outputs as final authoritative hazard products without expert verification.

## Citation Obligation

If LSDetector, LSDFormer, LSDSAM, model weights, or generated outputs contribute to any publication or report, cite the relevant SCI model papers. Use the LSDFormer paper for LSDFormer-based results and the LSDSAM paper for LSDSAM-based results.

## Data And Weights

Remote-sensing imagery, labels, and trained model weights may carry their own terms. This license does not override third-party data, imagery, or model-weight restrictions.
